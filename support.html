Keep your run going.

How do I play?

Tap Start Run, choose Arcade, Pure or Daily, and read the short guide. Pick your position size and leverage. LONG benefits from rising prices; SHORT benefits from falling prices. Open a position and tap Start Replay, or wait without a position. Close or reverse during the replay. The market ends after 120 candles, about 24 seconds at normal speed.

How do I find upgrades and bosses?

Choose Arcade. After the third completed market, continue from its result to choose one of the offered upgrades; the choice changes your toolkit for the rest of the run. Another choice follows every third market. Every tenth Arcade market is a historical volatility boss. Pure has no perks; Daily is a separate ten-market attempt.

Where is my saved run?

Tap Continue Run on the home screen. Leaving the app pauses replay. Trading actions save immediately; replay saves every five candles, so force-closing may replay up to four candles. Starting another run replaces the active run after confirmation; completed market history remains in Stats.

Why does the app say Market Pack Updated?

An unfinished run from an older market pack cannot continue after that pack is replaced. Completed markets, XP and discoveries remain. Start a new run when ready; the previous active run is replaced only when you tap Let's Play.

Which historical market is used?

The app replays WBTC/USDC pool prices from Ethereum swaps. WBTC is wrapped Bitcoin; USDC is a dollar-linked token. One-minute candles use pool prices at the end of completed Ethereum blocks. Minutes without swaps carry the previous close with zero volume. The game uses simplified fees and leverage, not real exchange execution. Data source and published license.

Why is Daily unavailable?

Daily allows one local official attempt per UTC day. A day starts at 00:00 UTC, which may differ from local midnight. Beginning a Daily Run uses the attempt; leaving it unfinished does not grant another attempt. Continue the saved Daily Run if it is still active.

Can I move progress to another device?

REKT has no account, cloud sync or progress export. Progress is local. Deleting the app or resetting progress can remove it permanently. To erase in-app progress, open Settings → Reset All Progress and confirm.

Does it need internet or purchases?

No. Market history is included in the app. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, wallet connections or real-money trades.

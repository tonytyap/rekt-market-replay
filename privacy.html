REKT / Privacy

Your game stays yours.

Effective October 20, 2026. This policy covers REKT — Market Replay, developed by Anton Tyapugin.

Data collection

The app does not collect or transmit personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, location access or real-money trading services. Historical market data is bundled in the app; playing does not contact an exchange.

Local progress and settings

Active runs, results, daily attempts, XP, discoveries and preferences are stored on your device. The developer has no server copy and cannot recover them. REKT does not implement cloud sync. Device backups may be handled by Apple according to your system settings.

Deletion

Use Settings → Reset All Progress to delete the app's local runs, daily scores, XP and discoveries. Preferences are separate. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior.

Support email

If you contact support, the email address and information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, private keys or financial information. You may ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to any legal retention requirements.

This website

This website does not include advertising, analytics scripts, forms or custom cookies. Its hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP address and browser details, and may set security cookies to deliver and secure the pages. Opening an email link uses your email provider.

Children and simulated trading

The game uses virtual balances with no monetary value. It does not accept deposits, provide cash-out or offer prizes. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

Policy changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. Contact developtonyt@icloud.com for privacy questions.
